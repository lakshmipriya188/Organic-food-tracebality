Recommendation

Use LightGBM as the primary model

It delivers the highest accuracy across Top-1, Top-3, and Top-5, plus the best F1 / Precision / Recall balance across all 100 product classes.

Consider XGBoost instead if the application needs well-calibrated probability outputs — e.g. showing a confidence percentage to business users or applying probability thresholds — since its log loss (0.0708) is significantly better than LightGBM's (0.1119).

Avoid RandomForest for this task — despite a reasonable Top-1 accuracy, its poorly calibrated probabilities (log loss of 1.1058) make it unsuitable wherever confidence scores are used downstream.
